AI Drug Recommendation System

Exploring how Artificial Intelligence can support healthcare information and clinical decision-making.

A simple college project using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

1. Introduction

An AI Drug Recommendation System is a software concept that uses Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques to analyze healthcare information and support qualified medical professionals.

Such systems may help organize medical information, identify patterns, and highlight safety concerns. They require reliable data and clinical validation before being used in real healthcare.

2. Objectives

Understand AI

Learn how AI concepts can be applied to healthcare information.

Analyze Data

Understand how computer systems process and classify information.

Improve Safety

Recognize the importance of medication safety and professional review.

3. How Does It Work?

Step 1: Input

Relevant healthcare information is collected with privacy safeguards.

Step 2: Processing

The system organizes and checks the quality of the information.

Step 3: Analysis

An appropriately validated model analyzes relevant data.

Step 4: Review

A qualified healthcare professional reviews the findings.

5. Advantages

  • Can help organize large amounts of healthcare information.
  • Can identify patterns in suitable datasets.
  • May support consistent information retrieval.
  • Can highlight information that needs professional review.
  • Provides a useful area for AI research and learning.

6. Limitations

  • Incorrect data can produce unreliable results.
  • AI models may contain bias.
  • Medication safety can be complex.
  • Patient privacy must be protected.
  • Professional oversight and clinical validation are essential.

7. Technologies Used

HTML

Creates the structure and content of the website.

CSS

Provides the colors, layout, and responsive design.

JavaScript

Makes the educational demonstration interactive.

Note: This prototype uses predefined rules and sample data. It does not use a trained machine-learning model or a medical database.

8. Conclusion

An AI Drug Recommendation System demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence may help organize healthcare information and support clinical decision-making. Responsible systems require accurate data, careful testing, privacy safeguards, and qualified professional review. AI should assist healthcare professionals rather than replace them.
